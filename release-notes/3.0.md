Locations for a PC that travels, profiles that switch screens only, a protected PC outlet, quick switches for accessories, and a taskbar kept auto-hidden.

Illustrated guide: **[jmmaupin.github.io/PC-Multi-Screens-Shelly](https://jmmaupin.github.io/PC-Multi-Screens-Shelly/)**.

## Install

Download `ShellyPCScreens-{version}.exe` and run it: it offers to update the installed copy, and asks for an administrator once. Running instances in every session close by themselves first.

The executable is not signed yet: on first launch, SmartScreen shows *Windows protected your PC* → *More info* → *Run anyway*.

## What's new

- **Locations.** A PC carried from one desk to another, with a power strip and screens of its own in the suitcase, now has a location per place: its devices, its PC outlet, its screens, its layout, its profiles. The application drives only the location the PC is at, recognised by that location's PC outlet drawing power; at none of them (its outlets draw nothing, or do not answer), it switches nothing, and the tray icon turns purple. Settings → Devices → *Location...* puts a device at a location, or creates one. Away from a desk, its power strips no longer slow anything down: devices are searched for in parallel at start and wake, and those of another location are left alone once the PC is located.
- **Profiles switch screens only.** Accessories (USB hubs, speakers, a lamp) and outlets whose type is not set stay as they are, whatever the profile; before, a profile cut every outlet it did not list. Accessories switched off by sleep still come back on wake, as they were.
- **Quick switches.** Tick *On/off switch in the quick menu* on an outlet (Settings → Outlets): it gets an on/off switch in the window of the profile shortcut. A click switches it at once; the window stays open.
- **The PC role is protected.** Ticking *Powers the PC itself* on another outlet used to take the protection away from the real one, and the next profile could cut the PC. The role now moves only to an outlet drawing like a running PC, and after a confirmation; the outlet losing it stays protected as *critical* while it still draws like a PC.
- **Keep the taskbar auto-hidden** (Settings → Outlets, *Windows options*): Windows sometimes turns auto-hide off when screens come and go; ticked, it is turned back on within seconds.

## Check after updating

- **The PC outlet must draw above the off threshold while the PC runs** (Settings → PC power). The application now relies on it to know the PC is there: below it, it believes the PC is elsewhere and switches nothing.
- **Profiles only switch outlets whose type is *Screen*.** If one of your screens is still *Not set* in Settings → Outlets, set it to *Screen*: until then, profiles leave it alone.

With a single location, the hardware configuration is left as it was: the update does not ask for an administrator to save it.
