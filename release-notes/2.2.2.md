First public release of Shelly PC Screens: control the power of a PC's screens and peripherals through Shelly Power Strip 4 Gen4 devices, from the Windows notification area.

Illustrated guide: **[jmmaupin.github.io/PC-Multi-Screens-Shelly](https://jmmaupin.github.io/PC-Multi-Screens-Shelly/)** (overview, every feature, step-by-step setup).

## Install

Download `ShellyPCScreens-{version}.exe` and run it. It installs for every account of the PC (the screens are shared), in `C:\Program Files\Shelly PC Screens`, and asks for an administrator once. Uninstall from *Settings → Apps → Installed apps*.

The executable is not signed yet: on first launch, SmartScreen shows *Windows protected your PC* → *More info* → *Run anyway*.

## Highlights

- Screen profiles from the tray menu or a global shortcut, with a screen map; never leaves every screen off.
- Power-based detection running on the power strip itself: screens follow the PC's sleep and boot, even with the application closed.
- Power consumption history, CSV export.
- Hardware settings are shared and need an administrator; each account keeps its own profiles.
- With several accounts signed in, only the session on the screen drives the power strips.

## Coming from the sources (1.x)

The installer finds the previous installation through its startup shortcut, imports its configuration and history, and removes the old shortcuts.

Tested on Windows 11 64-bit with a Shelly Power Strip 4 Gen4 (firmware 2.0.1-beta3).
